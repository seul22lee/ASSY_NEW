## How this shaped the ontology

1. **Modules follow function, not chapters.** Mott gathers keys, couplings and
   seals into one chapter; Shigley splits shaft components across Chapter 7 and
   distributes clutches, brakes, couplings and flywheels into Chapter 16. Neither
   grouping is a fact about machine elements, so `ontology/machine-elements/`
   is organised by the function a family delivers.
2. **Pilot topics were chosen from the overlap.** Every pilot topic in v0.1 is
   covered by both books, which is what makes cross-source alignment possible at
   all.
3. **Single-source topics are marked, not hidden.** Where only one book covers a
   topic, the ontology can hold claims but no `ev:ClaimAlignment`, and queries
   asking whether the books agree correctly return nothing rather than a
   fabricated consensus.
