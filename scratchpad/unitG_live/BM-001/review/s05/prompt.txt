GRAMMAR
-------
<expr> is exactly one of
  {"ref": "<parameter id>"}   - the id "PRM-NNNN" of a parameter you declare, never its symbol
  {"const": <number>, "unit": "<unit>"}
  {"op": "+" | "-" | "*" | "/", "args": [<expr>, <expr>, ...]}
  {"op": "-", "args": [<expr>]}   - negation
A constraint expression is
  {"relation": "==" | "<=" | ">=", "lhs": <expr>, "rhs": <expr>}
Units: lengths in mm, angles in deg - every constant in those units, never another; a dimensionless factor has unit "1".
A coordinate at a frame origin is the typed zero {"const": 0, "unit": "mm"} - a coordinate definition, not a dimension.

FRAMES. The arrangement frame the envelopes, regions and joint origins below are stated in is THE frame; every body frame coincides with it at zero joint coordinates. A feature is placed RELATIVE to a committed datum:
  {"datum": "<Joint | Envelope | FunctionalRegion | Feature id>", "offset": [<expr>, <expr>, <expr>], "axis": "+X" | "-X" | "+Y" | "-Y" | "+Z" | "-Z"}
  datum: WHERE the feature sits - a Joint (its located frame), the body's own Envelope (its centre), a FunctionalRegion (its centre), or an earlier feature of the same body (its frame). A datum locates; it does not orient;
  offset: optional, three <expr>s in mm along the arrangement axes, default zero;
  axis: HOW the feature is oriented - its own axis (a bore's, a pin's, a slide's, a face's normal) as a signed arrangement axis. Omitted at a joint it is the joint's, which is what a bore, a pin or a bearing wants; omitted against an envelope or a region it is the arrangement's +Z; omitted against a feature it is that feature's. You may state a different axis at a joint, and it means "located by this joint, oriented otherwise" - a face whose normal crosses the hinge line, a snap arm projecting across it. A JOINT THAT DECLARES NO AXIS (a FIXED joint, or one whose axis_direction is NONE) IS A LOCATION ONLY: it has no orientation to lend, so every feature placed against it MUST state its own axis.

WHAT A FEATURE IS FOR IS A SEPARATE RECORD. Neither the datum nor the axis says that a feature EMBODIES anything: a keeper sitting on a hinge axis permits no rotation, and a face named STOP restrains nothing by being named. To say which geometry embodies which upstream relation, write:
  "kinematic_realizations": [{"id": "KRL-NNNN", "realizes": "<Joint or ConstraintRelation id>", "participating_features": ["FEA-NNNN", ...]}]
  It carries NO axis, NO coordinate and NO list of degrees of freedom - the joint or the relation already states those and stays the authority. List EVERY feature that does the job, on EVERY side: two rails and two carriages embody one guided relation together, three pads bearing on three recess walls embody guidance with no guide part at all, and a rod in a plain bore may leave a rotation free on purpose. None of those is aligned with anything in particular, and none is refused.
BASIS NUMBERS. The arrangement's own numbers - envelope extents and centres, joint origins, region volumes - are in the ReferenceScale basis and are DIMENSIONLESS: written as constants they carry unit "1", never "mm". A length in mm that follows from one is scale x basis number: e.g. {"op": "*", "args": [{"ref": "<SCALE parameter>"}, {"const": 100, "unit": "1"}]}.
The feature frame has Z along its axis, origin at datum + offset, and X along the canonical perpendicular (Z->X, X->Y, Y->Z, positive); the feature's construction is built in it.

Operations (parameters are an object of named <expr>s; operands are ids of EARLIER steps of the same feature):
  BOX        operands: none; takes dx, dy, dz             the solid [0,dx] x [0,dy] x [0,dz] from the frame origin
  CUT        operands: two or more; parameters: none      the first operand with every later operand removed - all of them steps of THIS feature; never the body, which no step names
  CYLINDER   operands: none; takes radius, height         the solid of radius r, axis +Z of the frame from z=0 to z=height, centred on the frame origin in X and Y
  INTERSECT  operands: two or more; parameters: none      the common volume of the operands
  ROTATE     operands: exactly one; takes angle           the operand rotated by angle (degrees) about the named body axis through the frame origin
  SPHERE     operands: none; takes radius                 the solid of radius r centred on the frame origin
  TRANSLATE  operands: exactly one; takes dx, dy, dz      the operand moved by (dx, dy, dz) in the feature frame; an omitted component is zero
  UNION      operands: two or more; parameters: none      the operands fused - all of them steps of THIS feature
  ROTATE also names `axis`, one of X | Y | Z.
  A combining operation has no parameters of its own: what it combines is an earlier step OF THE SAME FEATURE, named as an operand.
  A feature never names another feature, or another feature's step, as an operand.

POLARITY, NOT A STEP.
  A FEATURE'S CONSTRUCTION BUILDS ITS OWN SOLID, and that solid is ALWAYS POSITIVE material - the shape of the thing itself.
  A bore is the cylinder that will be removed, constructed as a cylinder; a boss is the cylinder that will be added, constructed the same way.
  WHETHER IT IS ADDED OR REMOVED IS ITS KIND, not a step: the body is the union of its ADDITIVE features with its SUBTRACTIVE features removed, and that composition happens at the BODY, after every feature is built.
  CUT, UNION and INTERSECT are FEATURE-LOCAL CSG: they shape this feature's own solid out of two or more of ITS OWN earlier steps - a bore with a relief, a boss with a flat.
  Each needs at least two operands, because that is what combining two solids means.
  A SUBTRACTIVE feature must NOT write CUT to mean "remove this from the body": the body is not an operand, no step may name it, and its kind has already said so.

RESPONSE SCHEMA
Return a single JSON object with exactly these 6 keys, each holding a
list. A list may be empty - an empty list is a value. Every id is a string in the
format shown, where NNNN stands for a zero-padded number you choose. NNNN is a
FORMAT, not a value: write an actual number in its place, and never one listed
below as already in use.

  features[]               id "FEA-NNNN", body -> Body id, feature_kind in
                           {STOCK | BOSS | LUG | PIN | SHAFT | DOWEL | BORE |
                           HOLE | BEARING | GUIDE | SLOT | RAIL | FACE | RIB |
                           SHOULDER | STOP | CLEARANCE_POCKET | SNAP_ARM |
                           KEEPER}, geometry, interface (optional) -> Interface
                           id, placement {datum, offset?, axis?}, construction
                           [{id, operation, operands?, parameters?, axis?}]
  realizations[]           id "RLZ-NNNN", addresses_obligations -> Obligation
                           ids, participating_features -> Feature ids,
                           verification_predicate
  kinematic_realizations[] id "KRL-NNNN", realizes -> Joint |
                           ConstraintRelation id, participating_features ->
                           Feature ids
  parameters[]             id "PRM-NNNN", symbol, unit, role (optional) in
                           {SCALE}
  constraints[]            id "CON-NNNN", expression, parameters, kind, basis
                           (optional) in {GEOMETRIC_RELATION | STATED_BOUND |
                           DESIGN_CHOICE}, governs_interface (optional) ->
                           Interface id
  unresolved[]             id "S5U-NNNN", decision, why_open, alternatives,
                           alternatives_kind, kept_open_by -> Ambiguity |
                           Freedom ids, blocks -> ANY ids

  feature_kind   STOCK | BOSS | LUG | PIN | SHAFT | DOWEL | BORE | HOLE | BEARING | GUIDE | SLOT | RAIL | FACE | RIB | SHOULDER | STOP | CLEARANCE_POCKET | SNAP_ARM | KEEPER
    ADDITIVE     STOCK | BOSS | LUG | PIN | SHAFT | DOWEL | RAIL | FACE | RIB | SHOULDER | STOP | SNAP_ARM | KEEPER
    SUBTRACTIVE  BORE | HOLE | BEARING | GUIDE | SLOT | CLEARANCE_POCKET
  constraint kind ENVELOPE | CLEARANCE | DIMENSIONAL | INTERFERENCE_FREE
  operation      BOX | CUT | CYLINDER | INTERSECT | ROTATE | SPHERE | TRANSLATE | UNION
  placement axis +X | -X | +Y | -Y | +Z | -Z
  ROTATE axis    X | Y | Z   (required by ROTATE, and by nothing else)
