# AMB-001-3-01 — BM-001-3 resting stability

**Status:** PRESERVED. Non-blocking.
**Effect:** BM-001-3 remains evaluable; `NRM-BM-001-3-002` requires only that the
resting configuration be *derived*, and stability itself is `NOT_VERIFIED`.

REQ-011 asserts the enclosure "rests on a curved back" but supplies no stability
criterion, no tipping case and no support condition. A curved underside may rock
or roll. Whether "rests" implies static stability, or merely names the
orientation, is not determinable from rank-1 sources.

Not blocking because the Oracle can still separate compliant from non-compliant
designs on the parts that *are* determined: a curved underside must exist, and
the resting orientation must be derived from contact rather than named.
