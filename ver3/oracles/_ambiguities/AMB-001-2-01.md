# AMB-001-2-01 — BM-001-2 release-side tension

**Status:** PRESERVED. No resolution invented.
**Effect:** BM-001-2 is `BLOCKED_BY_SOURCE_AMBIGUITY` for lock. Authoring,
auditing and all other packs continue.

## The two rank-1 statements

- REQ-010 (BM-001-2): "The latch must not be releasable from the exposed side;
  **it can only be worked from inside the enclosure**."
- REQ-001 (BM-001): "The product must provide a mechanism for repeatedly opening
  and closing the storage box."
- REQ-003 (BM-001): "The product must have a latch that is easy for a user to
  operate."

## Why they cannot be reconciled without interpretation

A retention worked only from inside a *closed* enclosure cannot be operated to
open that enclosure. Taken together the three requirements describe an operation
whose precondition is its own result.

## Candidate readings — none selected

| # | Reading | What it would require |
|---|---|---|
| a | "inside" = enclosure interior; the retention is worked only when the enclosure is already open | REQ-001/REQ-003 apply to some other opening action |
| b | "inside" = interior of the wall; actuated through a port on a non-exposed side | REQ-010's "from the exposed side" is the only real prohibition |
| c | "inside" = the mounting side, accessible before mounting | the product is not user-openable once mounted |

`meta.notes[1]` ("the beam runs down the inside of the wall and the nose comes
out through it") describes **one Ver2 realization** at feature level. It is
rank 5 and does not settle a requirement-level tension.

## Why this blocks fair evaluation

An acceptance predicate must be able to separate a compliant from a
non-compliant design. Under reading (a) a design with no exterior actuator
complies; under (b) the same design fails unless it has a non-exposed-side port.
The readings disagree on the *same* design, so no predicate can be fair until a
reading is chosen by evidence.

## What would unblock it

A rank-1 clarification of "inside the enclosure", or an explicit statement of
how the enclosure is opened.
