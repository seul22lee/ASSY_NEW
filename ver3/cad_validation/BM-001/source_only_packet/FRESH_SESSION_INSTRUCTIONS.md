# Fresh-session instructions — BM-001 Demonstration CAD

You are the **Demonstration CAD author**. Read this packet and nothing else from
this repository.

## What you have

Six files in this directory: the verbatim rank-1 source, the precedence rule, the
ambiguity note, the generic artifact requirements, the status vocabulary and the
material-class policy.

## What you must not seek out

Do not open, search for, or ask about anything else in this repository —
in particular any acceptance criteria, evaluation rules, expected checks, or other
CAD.

If you encounter any of it by accident, **say so in your report**. A Demonstration
authored with knowledge of the acceptance criteria establishes nothing, and a
contaminated run that is declared contaminated is far more useful than one that
quietly is.

## What you are being asked

Design a product that satisfies the rank-1 source, and build it as parametric
B-rep CAD meeting the generic artifact requirements.

That is the whole task. There is no target shape. Two different authors should be
expected to produce two different products, and that is a success, not a
discrepancy.

## Why this is set up so awkwardly

The question this exercise exists to answer is whether the **source alone** is
enough to lead a competent author somewhere acceptable. Only a session that never
saw the acceptance criteria can test that.

If your design is accepted, the source carried the meaning. If it is rejected, the
interesting question is whether you made a mistake or whether the criteria demand
something the source never said — and that second case is a defect in the
criteria, not in you.

So: build what the source asks for. Do not try to guess what will be accepted.

## Recording your choices

Where the source says nothing — and it says nothing about a great deal — record:

1. what you chose,
2. that the source did not require it,
3. what else would have been equally admissible.

That record is the most valuable thing you will produce.

## When you are done

Write your model and artifacts into `../demonstrations/DEM-BM001-<n>/`.

Do not evaluate yourself against any acceptance criteria. You do not have them,
and inventing them would defeat the exercise. Report what you built and what you
assumed; someone else will do the evaluation.
